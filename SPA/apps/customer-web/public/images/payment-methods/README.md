# Payment method artwork

The four bank app icons are local, resized copies of the current artwork published by each bank on the Thailand App Store. They are used only as compact recognition cues beside the corresponding Beam payment method.

- `kplus.jpg`: [K PLUS](https://apps.apple.com/th/app/k-plus/id361170631), KASIKORNBANK PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED
- `scb-easy.jpg`: [SCB EASY](https://apps.apple.com/th/app/scb-easy/id568388474), The Siam Commercial Bank PCL
- `krungsri.jpg`: [krungsri](https://apps.apple.com/th/app/krungsri/id571873195), Bank of Ayudhya Public Company Limited
- `bangkok-bank.jpg`: [Bangkok Bank Mobile Banking](https://apps.apple.com/th/app/bangkok-bank-mobile-banking/id660238716), Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited

Keep the payment method name and bank name as accessible text; do not use the artwork as the only label.
